🔺 Triangle Connect 4

Isosceles & Equilateral Triangles • Two-player classroom review
🔴 Player 1🟡 Player 2

Answer the question correctly, then select a column to drop your piece.

Players alternate. Solve the displayed triangle problem. A correct answer unlocks one piece placement in any non-full column. An incorrect answer ends your turn. Connect four pieces in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. Enter numerical answers without units; decimals are accepted when appropriate.

Each round generates new problems practicing the same skills as the supplied worksheet. The diagram is illustrative and not drawn to scale.
